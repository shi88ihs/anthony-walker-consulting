## Context

The client needed practical on-site support during an office expansion. Remote planning had already identified the broad requirements, but the work still needed someone physically present to check cabling, place equipment, coordinate with stakeholders, and confirm that workstations were usable before staff moved in.

The scope included structured cabling checks, switch deployment, workstation setup, and clear documentation. The engagement was anonymised, and no client name, site address, or vendor-sensitive details are included here.

## On-Site Work

The first task was confirming the site matched the plan. Cable runs and wall ports were checked against the expected desk layout, and issues were raised before equipment was finalised. The switch deployment was coordinated around live business needs so the new office floor could come online without unnecessary disruption.

Workstations were configured, tested, and domain-joined where required. Each desk was checked for network access, basic peripheral readiness, and user handover requirements. When something did not match the plan, it was documented and escalated instead of being hidden as an informal workaround.

## Handover

The final documentation covered switch placement, port mapping, workstation status, outstanding notes, and practical next steps for the client's internal or remote IT team. The value of the work was not just getting hardware connected. It was leaving the site in a state where the next person could understand what had been done.
